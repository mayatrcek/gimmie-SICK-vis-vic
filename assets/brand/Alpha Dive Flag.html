Admiralty Log — Anno 1871

Old Snaggletooth

the colours bitten by a blind sevengiller

On a grey morning off the Cornish coast, the diving barque Prudence let her alpha colours trail in the swell while her men worked the bell below. From the murk rose a great sevengill shark, milk-eyed and blind, that knew the world only by scent and motion.

Mistaking the fluttering swallowtail for a wounded gull upon the water, the old beast closed its seven-gilled jaws and took the fly clean away. The crew, being Victorian gentlemen of considerable composure, simply noted the hour in the log and carried on.

The captain forbade any repair. A chewed swallowtail, he declared, was an honour earned — a mark that one had worked the deep where the blind sevengiller keeps its lightless watch. To this day the fleet flies its colours frayed, and calls it paying respects to Old Snaggletooth.
