Amazing: swell <1 m, good period (<13 s) & light wind (<15 km/h).   Good: swell <1 m but wind moderate or period a touch high.   Marginal: swell 1–1.5 m, or <1 m with very high period (≥14 s), or strong onshore wind.   Poor: swell >1.5 m.   (Heavy recent rain knocks a tier off; sheltered bay sites ignore swell.)

About this site

This is a personal dive- and fishing-conditions dashboard for the Victorian coast — from the Surf Coast and Port Phillip Heads across to Wilsons Promontory. It brings swell, wind, rainfall, sea-surface temperature, chlorophyll and seabed information together for the spots I actually dive, so I can tell at a glance whether tomorrow is worth an early start.

Everything here runs on free, public data: marine and weather forecasts from Open-Meteo, sea-surface temperature from NASA JPL MUR, chlorophyll from NOAA CoastWatch, seabed habitat from Seamap Australia, and high-resolution bathymetry and depth contours from DEECA CoastKit (Victoria). The whole thing is a single static web page — no account, no tracking, no servers of mine.

Who’s “us”: built by Maya — a first-year computer-science student who dives and fishes the Victorian coast — together with Anthropic’s Claude (model claude-opus-4-8), which helped write and wire up the maps, data feeds and conditions logic.

Ratings and seabed labels are indicative and for planning only. Always check official forecasts, tides and safety information before entering the water.

Got a suggestion, found a bug, or know a spot I should add? Let me know — or open the form in a new tab.
Forecasts: Open-Meteo (marine + weather models). SST: NASA JPL MUR. Chlorophyll: NOAA NESDIS CoastWatch (VIIRS), via NOAA ERDDAP. Free public data — a planning aid, not for navigation or safety-of-life decisions. Built for Maya.
